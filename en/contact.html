How do I contact the Employee Care Department?

Three ways. We recommend a ticket because it gives you a number to track your request.

1. Online ticket (best)

Fill in the form in three steps and get a number to track your request.

Open a ticketTrack a ticket

2. Email

R3-shqra-hrcs@moh.gov.sa

Send from your ministry email. Include your name, employee number, mobile, facility and request type.

Write an emailCopied

3. Phone

Ext. 671

Call the Employee Care Department on extension 671. Best for urgent matters.

What you need to open a ticket: Your ministry email ending in moh.gov.sa, your mobile number (05XXXXXXXX) and your employee number. These are required so the team can reach you.

What happens after you submit?

  1. Ticket numberShown right after you submit. Keep it: it is not sent by email at the moment.
  2. ReviewThe ticket goes to the department's dashboard. They may contact you on your ministry email or mobile.
  3. StatusIt changes between: New, In progress, Closed.
  4. ReplyIt appears on the Track page when you enter your ticket number and ministry email.
No reply time has been announced, so we cannot promise one. For urgent matters call extension 671.

Request types

  • Inquiry A question about a procedure or your job status. Try the FAQ first.
  • Complaint A comment on a procedure or treatment that needs follow-up.
  • Suggestion An idea to improve the transition or staff services.
  • Information request A request for a specific statement or piece of information.
FAQ · Privacy: ticket data is used only to contact you about your request, and is seen by the Employee Care Department. Do not send passwords, bank details or ID numbers. How long data is kept will be agreed with the department.
