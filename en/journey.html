1. Before your transfer request

What decides whether you receive a transfer request, and what to prepare now.

  • Your right

    A transfer request is sent to every staff member who passed the selection criteria. You can accept or decline.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5696

  • Your right

    Criteria: for staff under the Health Regulation: qualifications, experience, performance evaluation and SCFHS classification. For administrative staff: qualifications, experience and performance evaluation.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · SCFHS: practitioner FAQ · The question in our FAQ: 5723

  • Your right

    The job offer is under your current job title.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5724

  • Your right

    If you meet the criteria you can choose to transfer or not (not transferring has the paths shown in stage 3).

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5700

  • Your right

    Workers (including self-operated and wage-item staff) are treated under the worker provisions of Cabinet Decision 616: a contract with basic and total pay not lower than before, continuous social-insurance contributions, and continuous service for end-of-service and leave.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5704 5728

  • Your right

    Staff employed by operating companies or hospital subsidiaries are not currently a targeted group; their contract follows their company.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5708

  • Your right

    If you are on a study scholarship: they will contact you after you return and resume work, to arrange the transfer according to your new qualification.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5718

  • You do

    Update your data in Mawared. The transfer request is emailed to your ministry email based on what is recorded there.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · MOH: Mawared app · The question in our FAQ: 5710

  • You do

    Check that your status is “Active” in Mawared. Requests are currently sent to staff shown as Active; if you are on leave and shown as Inactive you will not receive one.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5764

  • You do

    Use your leave balance before signing the contract, especially compensatory leave: those balances are not carried over after signing.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5757 5754

  • You do

    Civil-service employees get a grace period of up to 60 days between accepting the transfer request and the contract taking effect, to use annual or compensatory leave, without affecting work requirements.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 8544

No items in this stage for your selection. Ask the Employee Care Department.

2. When the transfer request and contract arrive

What the contract gives you, and what you must do to accept or decline.

  • Your right

    Your basic pay and total pay will not be lower than before the transfer. The contract includes housing and transport allowances only, ensuring total pay does not drop.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5683 5681

  • Your right

    The contract lasts two years and renews automatically for a similar term unless either party gives notice of non-renewal. There is no probation period.

    Nationality note (from an official text): Non-Saudi: Article 37 of the Labor Law requires a non-Saudi's employment contract to be written and fixed-term. Labor Law – Bureau of Experts

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5765 5767

  • Your right

    The only deduction linked to the transfer request is the statutory social-insurance deduction.

    Nationality note (from an official text): Non-Saudi: covered by the occupational-hazards branch only; not registered in the pension branch. GOSI: contributor FAQ

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5693

  • Your right

    Transfer bonus: 16% of basic pay for each year of service (pro-rata for part of a year), capped at four basic salaries, or the end-of-service award under your previous employment system, whichever is higher.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5744 5742

  • Your right

    Leave-balance compensation: balance from 1/7/1439H is compensated up to 72 days, earlier balance up to 180 days, and the total never exceeds 180 days. Any unpaid dues (assignments, allowances…) are paid within the end-of-service procedures.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5755 5691 5705

  • Your right

    Your service is continuous for leave and end-of-service, and your full leave balance is carried over (for self-operated staff, per the official FAQ).

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5755 5728

  • Your right

    With 25+ years: a contract is offered if you pass the criteria regardless of years of service. If you accept the transfer you can choose between merging your previous service into your new service (benefit exchange) or early retirement.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5726 5713 5702

  • Your right

    Note: someone who chooses early retirement cannot sign a new employment contract with the health cluster.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5721

  • Your right

    With under 25 years: you can choose to merge your previous service (civil retirement) into your new GOSI service. Your dues and the transfer bonus are paid, and the pension is calculated under the benefit-exchange system.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5705

  • You do

    Open Qiwa Individuals → Services → Employment contracts, review the contract sent to you, then accept or decline. You will receive a text message from Qiwa to document the contract.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · Qiwa: reviewing new work contracts · The question in our FAQ: 5769 5709

  • You do

    Read the contract carefully before replying: per the official FAQ the details of the contract sent cannot be edited, and the decision is accept or decline on the uploaded wording. (Qiwa generally offers a request-modification option for work contracts; the edit is made by the employer.)

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · HRSD: contract management (documentation) · The question in our FAQ: 5770

  • You do

    Your reply is final: a refusal cannot be reversed, and the Decision 616 procedures start as soon as you accept or decline. Reply within the period stated in your own transfer request.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5711

  • You do

    If you choose to merge your service: who submits the merge request to GOSI, and how, is not published in the sources we reviewed; ask the Employee Care Department. Keep GOSI's published general merge conditions in mind: no award or pension already taken for the period, the period to merge at least one year, age not above 59 at the time of the request, and the pension not granted because of disability. We did not find text saying whether all of them apply to the transfer case specifically; ask the Employee Care Department.

    Source: GOSI: contributor FAQ · GOSI: benefit-exchange system

No items in this stage for your selection. Ask the Employee Care Department.

3. If you choose not to transfer

The path depends on your job status and years of service. The decision is final.

  • Your right

    You may transfer your post to another government entity, in coordination with HRSD and the Ministry of Finance, within no more than 6 months from the ministry's acknowledgment of your request and notice of its decision (extendable for a similar period with the Minister's approval, in the narrowest limits). During this period you may be absent one full day or eight hours a week to look for an entity.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5699

  • Your right

    With 25+ years: you can choose between early retirement under the regulations or transferring your post to another government entity within 6 months, as above.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5735 5702

  • Your right

    If you do not move within the period, your dues are settled and your service in the post ends.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5699

  • Your right

    A Labor-Law worker who met the criteria and does not wish to transfer: the contractual relationship ends and dues are settled under the regulations. The Labor Law requires settlement within one week if the employer ends the contract, two weeks if the worker does (Art. 88).

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · Labor Law – Bureau of Experts · The question in our FAQ: 5722 5735

  • You do

    Do not delay: the decision cannot be reopened. If in doubt, ask the Employee Care Department before replying.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5711

No items in this stage for your selection. Ask the Employee Care Department.

4. After the transfer

Your rights under the new contract and the Labor Law, and what to follow up.

  • Your right

    30 calendar days of annual leave, excluding official public holidays. (The Labor Law minimum is 21 days, 30 after five continuous years.)

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · Labor Law – Bureau of Experts · The question in our FAQ: 5758

  • Your right

    An annual raise based on performance evaluation, as a percentage of basic pay; housing and transport allowances rise with it, which feeds into end-of-service and pension. The annual evaluation is 360-degree. The excellence allowance was replaced by an annual reward for outstanding staff.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5746 5743 5688

  • Your right

    Medical insurance: covers treatment for you, your family and your parents inside the health cluster network and affiliated networks. We did not find the coverage limits and details on an official page; ask the Employee Care Department.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5741

  • Your right

    While your contract is still valid with the Ministry of Health, current allowance policies apply, per entitlement before you sign; after the transfer, the health cluster's policies apply.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5694

  • Your right

    End-of-service under the Labor Law: half a month's pay per year for the first five years and one month per year after, based on the last wage. On resignation: one third after two years, two thirds after five, full after ten or more. Your other rights: sick leave (30 days full pay, then 60 at three quarters, then 30 unpaid), 8 hours a day and 48 a week (6 hours a day in Ramadan for Muslims).

    Nationality note (from an official text): Non-Saudi: Articles 84–88 address “the worker” without distinguishing nationality. Article 40 obliges the employer to bear residence and work-permit fees and renewals, and the return ticket home after the relationship ends. Labor Law – Bureau of Experts

    Source: Labor Law – Bureau of Experts

  • Your right

    Your previous service counts toward early retirement: official example: someone with 20 years before transferring needs only 5 more years to qualify for early retirement.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 8543 5713

  • Your right

    Service periods merged because of transformation or privatization can qualify for a pension before age 60 (an official exception). However, the pension on such merged periods may not be combined with a salary or wage.

    Source: GOSI: benefit-exchange FAQ · The question in our FAQ: GOSI-2 GOSI-3

  • Your right

    Early retirement in GOSI: someone under 60 may request it with at least 300 months (25 years) of actual contribution and having left work covered by the system. GOSI amended the system (from 3 July 2024) to raise the statutory retirement age and early-retirement period gradually for a defined group by age and contribution period (age 58–65, 25–30 years): check your case with GOSI.

    Source: GOSI: contributor FAQ · GOSI: system amendments (3 July 2024)

  • You do

    Make sure the Qiwa message arrived and your contract is documented; keep a copy.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · HRSD: contract management (documentation) · The question in our FAQ: 5709

  • You do

    Any approved leave starting after you sign is counted from the new balance, and you must submit a new leave request.

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5760

  • You do

    If you notice a difference in your leave balance or pay, ask the Employee Care Department. (Leave balances were updated by a periodic review unrelated to the transfer.)

    Source: Official FAQ – Health Holding · The question in our FAQ: 5762

No items in this stage for your selection. Ask the Employee Care Department.
