What do I need to do now?

  1. Update your data in MawaredYour transfer request is built from the data saved there.
  2. Watch your ministry emailThe transfer request goes to staff who passed the selection criteria and whose status is “Active” in Mawared.
  3. Review your contract in QiwaYou will get a message from Qiwa. Then go to: Qiwa Individuals → Services → Employment contracts.
