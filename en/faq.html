What does transition mean?

It is the move of the employees of a government entity from the Civil Service Law, or any other employment system, to the Labor Law; or the change of their workers' coverage from the employment regulation applied to them to another employment regulation, as a result of the transition decision issued by the Council of Ministers approving the transition.

Source: Health Holding – FAQ (Arabic original)

What does privatization mean?

It is the transfer of the affiliation of the employees and workers of the Ministry of Health from the public sector to the Health Holding Company, which is a company that has an independent legal personality and independent financial accountability, and is fully owned by the State, as a result of the issuance of the privatization decision.

Source: Health Holding – FAQ (Arabic original)

Will the Health Holding Company replace the Ministry of Health?

No, the Health Holding Company will not replace the Ministry of Health. The company will take over the operational side that the Ministry used to carry out before, while the Ministry will remain responsible for the regulatory and supervisory aspects.

Source: Health Holding – FAQ (Arabic original)

What is the Health Holding Company and what is its work?

The Health Holding Company is a company fully owned by the State, which has an independent legal personality and independent financial accountability. It takes over the provision of health care through Health Clusters affiliated with it, which provide comprehensive and integrated health care within their defined geographic areas. It was established under Council of Ministers Decision No. (469) dated 19/8/1443 AH.

Source: Health Holding – FAQ (Arabic original)

What are the Health Clusters?

A Health Cluster is a unified institutional system that has an integrated and interconnected network of facilities providing health care services. The Health Cluster is the entity responsible for everything that affects the health of the community and individuals within its defined geographic area, where it works to support a living environment that promotes health, and provides comprehensive health care services of all kinds, with a focus on the quality of the services and not only on the facilities that provide them.

Source: Health Holding – FAQ (Arabic original)

The number of Health Clusters is currently 20 Health Clusters. There is no plan at the present time to increase the number of Health Clusters.

What is meant by an employee?

What is meant by a worker?

It is every natural person who works for the benefit of a government entity or an administrative entity and under its management, and is subject to the Labor Law and the GOSI (General Organization for Social Insurance) system. This is what is referred to in the Ministry as self-operated staff.

A worker may not actually be employed for more than eight hours a day if the employer adopts the daily standard, or more than forty-eight hours a week if the employer adopts the weekly standard. The actual working hours are reduced during Ramadan for Muslims so that they do not exceed six hours a day or thirty-six hours a week. (Labor Law, Article Ninety-Eight). This is a general text in the Law, and your specific working hours are stated in the transition request.

Source: Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (HRSD)

How is the end-of-service benefit calculated under the Labor Law?

If the work relationship ends, the employer pays the worker a benefit for his period of service calculated on the basis of half a month's wage for each of the first five years, and one month's wage for each of the following years, and the last wage is taken as the basis for the calculation, and the benefit is due for parts of a year in proportion to the time spent in work. (Labor Law, Article Eighty-Four). This is a general text, and the details of your case are determined by the systems applied to you and the approved answers in the "Rewards and Benefits" section.

Source: Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (HRSD)

What is the statutory period for settling a worker's rights at the end of his service?

If the worker's service ends, the employer must pay his wage and settle his rights within one week at most from the date the contractual relationship ends; but if it is the worker who ended the contract, his rights must be settled in full within a period not exceeding two weeks. (Labor Law, Article Eighty-Eight).

Source: Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (HRSD)

What is meant by the benefits-exchange system?

The benefits-exchange system between the Civil and Military Pension systems and the GOSI system is a system that allows a subscriber who is subject to the Civil Pension system or the Military Pension system and has a prior subscription period in the GOSI system, or vice versa, to add his subscription period in the first system to his subscription period in the latter system, in order to benefit from those periods when settling his retirement dues.

Source: GOSI – FAQ on amending the benefit-exchange system

Do the added periods qualify for entitlement to a retirement pension before reaching the age of sixty?

The subscription periods added between the GOSI system and the Civil Pension system or the Military Pension system do not qualify for entitlement to a retirement pension before reaching the age of sixty, as the subscriber must complete the period required by the latter system to obtain that pension. As an exception to that, the added periods can be used for entitlement to the pension before reaching the age of sixty if the reasons for the addition result from transition or privatization, or if the end of service is due to death, disability, or being removed from service through coordination.

Source: GOSI – FAQ on amending the benefit-exchange system

Is it permissible to combine the retirement pension due for the added periods resulting from transition or privatization with the salary or wage?

In the case of an addition resulting from transition or privatization, it is not permissible to combine the retirement pension due for the added periods resulting from transition or privatization with the salary or wage.

Source: GOSI – FAQ on amending the benefit-exchange system
